My favourite thing that I have read is Andy Weir's Project Hail Mary.

The novel speaks about our main character, Dr. Ryland Grace as he travels the stars to complete this last-chance mission save the world. Except, he doesn't know that.

Without spoilers; this heartfelt emotional journey across the stars was an entertaining, emotional, and inspiring read. Both the book and the movie is grounded in real science, although it still is a hard sci-fi universe.

This novel has since been transformed into an award-winning film. Though, many critics say that the original book is superior.

I personally believe that both the movie and the novel are amazing works of art, and both deserve to be celebrated. I highly recommend both the book and the movie, neither of which are necessary to fully understand the other. Whichever medium you decide to consume Project Hail Mary, I can guarantee you will be charmed and entertained by it.

The book (and the movie) are both simply amaze.
